# Asset sources and permissions

## Wiko exterior photograph

- Local files: `assets/img/wiko-berlin.jpg` and
  `assets/img/wiko-berlin-small.jpg`.
- Original: [GrunewaldWallotstraße.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GrunewaldWallotstra%C3%9Fe.JPG), Wikimedia Commons.
- Photographer: Fridolin freudenfett (Peter Kuley).
- Photograph date: 21 April 2010. The site caption identifies it as a 2010 photograph.
- Selected licence: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- Changes: resized from 2560 × 1920 to 1440 × 1080 and 720 × 540,
  JPEG compression, and removal of metadata. No compositional or colour edits.
- These derivatives are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 licence.
  The contact-page caption credits the author, links to the original and
  licence, and states that the image was resized and compressed.
- The photograph is stored locally; it is not hotlinked.

Wiko's own [press-photo terms](https://www.wiko-berlin.de/presse/pressefotos)
restrict use to reporting about Wiko, require attribution and notification, and
limit image editing. Its building-history photograph has a copyright credit
without an explicit general reuse licence. A clearly licensed Commons
photograph was therefore selected instead.

already in this repository. Their existing rights remain unchanged. Original
