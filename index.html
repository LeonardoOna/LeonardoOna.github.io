Theoretical biology

I am a theoretical biologist studying ecological and evolutionary questions. I study how interactions among organisms alter the conditions under which natural selection acts, and how the resulting feedbacks shape cooperation, conflict, persistence, and biological organization.

Currently a Fellow at Wissenschaftskolleg zu Berlin, College for Life Sciences.

Research

My research centers on three connected problems: interdependence and cooperation, conflict and coevolution, and ecological and evolutionary feedbacks. A fuller description is available on the About page.
